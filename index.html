About MDB

Michael Okeoghene Okomunu, professionally known as MDB, is a Nigerian gamer, livestreamer, and content creator born in Warri, Delta State, Nigeria.

Known within the global Call of Duty: Mobile community, MDB creates livestreams, gaming highlights, tutorials, and entertainment-driven content for audiences across Africa and beyond.

MDB is of Isoko descent from Southern Nigeria and studied Agricultural Engineering at the University of Benin.
